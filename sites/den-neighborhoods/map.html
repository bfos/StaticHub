Denver Neighborhoods Map

See how each neighborhood sits in relation to downtown, to each other, and to Denver's parks and amenities

This map shows all ten neighborhoods in this guide as colored zones overlaid on Denver's street grid. Click any neighborhood to jump to its detail page. Hover over a zone to see the neighborhood name, median price, and walk score.

The scale of Denver may surprise you — the city is physically large but the neighborhoods in this guide cluster in a fairly tight band around the city center. Downtown Denver is the reference point at center. The neighborhoods span roughly 10 miles north-to-south and 8 miles east-to-west, but most are within a 10–20 minute drive of each other.
