How this works
What counts as a rebook
Airlines
Delta, American, United, Alaska, Southwest, Aeroméxico, WestJet and Air Canada, one airline both ways on one ticket. Budget carriers (Spirit, Frontier, Allegiant, Volaris, Viva Aerobus), mixed-airline trips and Kayak Hacker Fares are ignored.
Fare class
Refundable fares only, because the booked ticket is refundable. The price compared is the cheapest tier Google Flights marks “Full refunds” — United Economy Fully Refundable, for example. Basic economy is excluded.
Qualifying flights
Leave MCI 15 March, leave PVR 20 March, at most one stop each way. Any time of day, any length. The five cheapest refundable fares, plus the booked Delta flight whenever it is listed. Flights with no refundable price fill any spare places, shown grey.
Sources
Refundable prices come from Google Flights alone: Kayak lists no refundable fares. Kayak’s regular fares appear only on grey flights, tagged K, and never count.
Stale flights
Only flights found in the latest check count toward the verdict. One that stops appearing keeps its last price, and after a day it is marked stale. Nothing is ever carried forward.
