MCI → SDQ rebook watch

If you split the groups

Each group priced on its own. A saving here is only real if you are willing to fly to Santo Domingo on different flights.

How this works
What counts as a rebook
Both flights are refundable, so any qualifying option below what was paid — $1,670 Girls, $1,560 Jen/Brian, $3,230 together — is worth acting on. There is no minimum margin.
Flying together
Both groups strongly prefer the same outbound flight to Santo Domingo, so the headline verdict pairs candidates that share an outbound and compares the combined cost. The returns differ by design: May 16 and May 20.
Airlines
American and Delta only. Other carriers are ignored, even when cheaper.
Fare class
Main Cabin or higher on American, Delta Main Classic or higher on Delta. Basic economy is excluded.
Qualifying times
Arrive SDQ 9:00 AM–2:00 PM · depart SDQ after 1:00 PM into MCI before midnight · under 11 hours each way. Five cheapest per itinerary.
Stale flights
If a flight's schedule shifts more than 20 minutes or it stops appearing, its last price is kept and marked stale, and it is left out of the verdict. Nothing is ever carried forward.
