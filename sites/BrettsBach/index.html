Right Around Arch Cape

Reality check: Arch Cape is a quiet residential pocket — no restaurants, just one vintage store on Hwy 101. You'll drive for food and nightlife (Cannon Beach ~5–8 min north, Manzanita ~12 min south). But what's walkable from the house is the best part: a wild, uncrowded beach and a hidden gem just up the sand.

WALKABLEArch Cape beach

right out the door · at low tide

The normally stony beach reveals packed sand at low tide — miles of quiet walking, beachcombing, and agate hunting with almost no crowds. The perfect coffee-in-hand morning stroll or sunset wander.

⭐ NEARLY WALKABLEHug Point State Recreation Site

~1 mile north of Arch Cape · low tide only

A standout and barely a mile up the beach: a cove with a waterfall that drops right onto the sand, sea caves, tide pools, and a historic wagon road carved into the headland (cross only at low tide). Genuinely magical and far less trafficked than Haystack Rock. Walk the beach there at low tide or make the 2-minute drive.

Free. Check the tide chart — the point is impassable at high tide.

Food & Gluten-Free

Cannon Beach punches well above its size on food. The bachelor is celiac-safe at several of these — flagged below.

FOODNewmans at 988 · GF on request

988 S Hemlock St · French-Italian fine dining · the special-occasion dinner

Intimate 10-table house — the town's standout splurge. Choose à la carte or the Chef's 4-course prix fixe with wine pairings. Perfect for the one "nice dinner" of the trip. They'll accommodate gluten-free with notice.

~$50–70 entrées; prix fixe ~$85–110 + pairings. Reservations essential — only 24–30 seats; book weeks ahead and note GF + party of 7. (503) 436-1151.

FOODThe Wayfarer Restaurant & Lounge · GF menu

1190 Pacific Dr · seasonal NW seafood · Haystack Rock views

Local-and-visitor favorite with the best ocean view in town — Dungeness crab, razor clams, chanterelles, grilled steaks. Great for a relaxed group dinner where the bachelor has real choices. The lounge is a solid lower-key option too.

Entrées ~$28–55. Reservations recommended for 7, especially a window table at sunset.

FOODDriftwood Restaurant & Lounge · GF-aware

179 N Hemlock St · classic steak & seafood · long-running local institution

A Cannon Beach fixture for decades — old-school, friendly, full of locals. Reliable steaks and seafood, lively lounge. Good "first night, ease in" dinner.

Entrées ~$25–48. Reservations a good idea for a group.

FOODPizza A'fetta

231 N Hemlock St · wood-fired pizza & salads · casual

Beloved two-floor pizzeria, generous portions, fresh ingredients. Great casual group lunch/dinner. (Note: not a dedicated GF kitchen — pair with another spot for the bachelor.)

Pies ~$20–32. Walk-in friendly; can wait at peak times.

🌾 For the bachelor: Newmans (with notice), The Wayfarer (GF menu), and Pelican Brewing (dedicated fryer, rice-flour fried items — see Nightlife) are the safest celiac bets. Suzy's Scoops is the locals' pick for careful GF ice cream / cross-contamination protocols.

Cozy Coffee Shops

You care about vibe and seating over a perfect pour — and never a chain. These are the independent, curl-up-with-a-book spots, enough to hit a new one every morning. They're spread across Cannon Beach, Manzanita (south), and Seaside/Gearhart (north) — so pick by which direction you're headed that day. (Note: Sleepy Monk's storefront was reported closed to the public, though it still "looks open" online — call ahead if you want to try it.)

☕Bald Eagle Coffee House ⭐ · best vibe

1064 S Hemlock St, Cannon Beach · independent, 30+ years

Exactly your kind of place. Family-owned for decades, walls full of eclectic photographs, musical instruments, and books, big sunny windows, and a co-located arts association so there's original art to browse while you sip. Lounge chairs and tables, homemade soups/pastries, dog-friendly garden patio. The most "settle in and stay a while" room in town. (Honest note: they pour Starbucks-brand beans — but it's a genuine independent shop, not a chain outlet, and the atmosphere is the draw.)

$4–9. Walk-in; good seating for a group.

☕Insomnia Coffee Co — Cannon Beach

one block from the beach · cozy roastery

Warm, lots of natural light, and a fireplace you can curl up next to with a book on a gray October morning — very on-brand for the bachelor. Signature Aztec Mocha, pastries, free Wi-Fi, dog-friendly. One caveat: seating is limited, so a group of 7 may want to split up or grab to-go and walk the beach.

$4–8. Walk-in.

☕Manzanita Coffee (Manzanita)

Laneda Ave, Manzanita · ~12 min south of Arch Cape

Steps from the beach on Manzanita's walkable main street, this little independent captures the laid-back North Coast soul — consistently excellent espresso and great scones. Worth folding into a Manzanita / Cape Falcon day. Pair with the gift shops and grocery along Laneda.

$4–7. Walk-in.

☕Controversial Coffee (Seaside) ⭐

downtown Seaside · ~20 min north · queer-owned

Full of personality and a genuine local favorite — a queer-owned shop with a creative seasonal menu and handcrafted drinks (and it pours Sleepy Monk beans, so you get that roast either way). A great, welcoming fit for your mixed crew and an easy stop on a Seaside or Astoria day.

$4–8. Walk-in.

☕Seaside Coffee Roasters (Seaside)

5 N Holladay Dr, Seaside · ~20 min north · in-house roaster

A relaxed, living-room atmosphere — homey decor that feels like a home away from home, brewing their own Seaside Coffee Roasting beans. Comfortable seating and exactly the cozy vibe you're after.

$4–7. Walk-in.

☕Burly & the Bean (Seaside)

Seaside · independent roastery · hands-on roast option

Independent roaster that grew from a drive-through into a proper shop. The fun twist: they offer a ~45-minute hands-on roasting experience that ends with a tasting of your own roast plus a bag to take home — a memorable group activity, not just a cup.

Coffee $4–7; roast experience higher — reserve ahead.

☕Brew 22 Coffee (Gearhart)

downtown Gearhart, behind King Tide bakery · ~25 min north

A tucked-away hidden gem in tiny, charming Gearhart — strong, bold, full-flavored drinks some call the best in the area. Grab a pastry from the King Tide bakery in front and you've got a perfect low-key morning.

$4–7. Walk-in.

☕Coffee Girl (Astoria)

Pier 39, Astoria · ~35 min north · over the river

If you do the Astoria day, this is the coffee stop — a beloved café built out on a historic cannery pier, with windows right over the Columbia River and ships drifting past. The view is the whole vibe. Pairs with the Astoria riverwalk and Column.

$4–7. Walk-in.

Wine — My Recommendation

You said "either, you recommend." Here's the honest read: serious Oregon wine (Pinot country) is in the Willamette Valley, ~90 min inland. The coast has charming tasting rooms but no real vineyards. Do both, on different days.

DAY TRIPWillamette Valley private wine tour ★ recommended

Dundee/Newberg area · ~90 min from coast (or do it on the PDX travel day)

This is the move for a group that loves wine. Hire a private driver/van so all 7 can drink — Pinot Noir is the signature grape, plus excellent Chardonnay and sparkling. Smartest logistics: do it the day you fly in or fly out, since the valley is between PDX and the coast — you're driving past it anyway. Round-tripping from Arch Cape adds ~3 hrs of driving, so pairing it with the airport day is the efficient play.

Vehicle for 7: One Sprinter van seats your whole group comfortably (most operators' vans hold 11–14). Luxury-SUV operators top out around 5–6 passengers, so you'd need two cars and split up — go with a van.

WINEThe Wine Shack & Puffin Tasting Room

124 N Hemlock St, Cannon Beach · open daily from noon

Town institution since 1977, best NW wine selection on the coast. The Puffin tasting room pours flights of their own label plus wine/beer/cider by the glass. Easy, walkable, no car needed — great late-afternoon hangout.

Flights ~$15–25. Walk-in; reserve only for a seated group of 7.

WINEThe Winery at Manzanita

387 Laneda Ave, Manzanita · ~20 min south

Cozy coastal wine bar + tasting room pairing wine with food, in laid-back Manzanita. Nice combine with the Oswald West / Cape Falcon hike, which is right between Cannon Beach and Manzanita.

Tastings ~$15–20. Walk-in friendly.

WINEVineside Wine Bar (Astoria)

Astoria · ~35 min north

Cozy local wine bar — NW standouts alongside French/Italian classics. Worth it if you do an Astoria day (see Off-the-wall).

By the glass / flights. Walk-in.

Outdoors

October on this coast is moody and gorgeous — bring layers and rain shells. All of these are short, scenic, and group-friendly.

HIKEEcola State Park — Clatsop Loop / Indian Beach

~10 min north of town · the iconic view

The postcard of the Oregon coast: cliff-top viewpoints over Tillamook Head, rainforest, and the lighthouse offshore. Clatsop Loop is ~2.5 mi round trip. Crescent Beach trail (also ~2.5 mi) drops to a secluded beach that stays empty even in peak season.

$5 per vehicle day-use. No reservation.

HIKECape Falcon — Oswald West State Park

~15 min south · best old-growth + ocean combo

2.3 mi each way through old-growth Sitka spruce to a dramatic headland with views over Smuggler's Cove and Short Sand Beach. The locals' pick for "real" forest. Pair with Short Sand Beach (½-mi walk, year-round surfers) and the Manzanita winery afterward.

Free. Lot fills midday on nice days — go earlier.

EXPERIENCEHaystack Rock private tide-pool tour ⭐

on the beach in town · low-tide only · Mar–Oct

Genuinely special and right in your wheelhouse: the Haystack Rock Awareness Program runs private guided tours for groups of 1–7 — exactly your size. Wade into the marine garden, meet sea stars, anemones, and puffins/cormorants overhead while a naturalist explains the ecosystem and protection history.

$35/person · 90 min (discount for 7+). Book ahead and they'll match it to the low-tide window. Check the Oct 2–5 tide chart when booking.

EXPERIENCESurf lessons — Indian Beach or Short Sands

Cannon Beach Surf Lessons & Rentals · 1.5–2 hr

A fun, bonding "we actually did the thing" morning. Private group lessons available; full wetsuits make October totally doable. Taught at Indian Beach (Ecola) or Short Sands (Oswald West).

~$75–125/person depending on group size. Reserve in advance.

Books, Art & Design

Tailored for the bachelor — Cannon Beach is officially one of America's "100 Best Art Towns," and the whole village is a study in good coastal design.

BOOKSJupiter's Rare & Used Books ⭐

244 N Spruce St · across from Downtown City Park

The bachelor will love this one. A small, unpretentious, deeply literary used/rare bookshop where owner Watt Childress holds court and talks books with you. Reasonable prices, real treasures. Easily an hour of happy browsing.

Free to browse.

BOOKSCannon Beach Book Company

130 N Hemlock St · the classic browser's bookstore

Hand-picked literary fiction, strong regional/Pacific NW section, deep mystery shelves. A great "buy the groom a beautiful book" stop.

ARTHemlock Street galleries + live glassblowing

downtown · self-guided gallery stroll

The downtown core is dense with galleries, public art, flower-filled courtyards, and coastal-modern architecture. You can catch live glassblowing and wander a dozen galleries in an afternoon. Architecture/design lovers should just walk Hemlock slowly.

FOOD+ARTFlavors of Cannon Beach guided walk

~3.5 hr · downtown

Guided walking tour blending galleries, shops, and tastings at the town's best restaurants — a tidy way to combine the food and art interests in one outing. Reserve ahead.

Nightlife & Drinks

The bachelor's not big on breweries, so this skews toward cocktail rooms, a whiskey bar, and a wine-bar-with-live-music — the "party a little" energy without a taproom vibe. (We've kept one brewery, Pelican, purely because it's the safest gluten-free spot in town.)

WHISKEY BARMacGregor's Whiskey Bar ⭐

heart of Cannon Beach · 200+ brown spirits

The standout grown-up drinking spot in town: craft cocktails plus 200+ whiskeys and brown liquors from around the world, in a warm, characterful room. Perfect for a relaxed-but-celebratory group night that isn't about beer. Spirits-forward, so good for the bachelor.

Cocktails ~$12–16. Walk-in; arrive earlier for a group of 7.

COCKTAILSThe Wayfarer Lounge · GF-friendly

at the Stephanie Inn · oceanfront · fireplace

The Wayfarer's lounge is the cozy-upscale option — quality mixology, a new whiskey bar, regional wines and premium spirits, a fireplace, and an ocean view. Slightly later hours and a full menu, so it doubles as a night-owl anchor after dinner. (Same building as the Wayfarer dinner spot.)

Cocktails ~$13–17.

WINE + MUSICSweet Basil's Café wine bar

downtown Cannon Beach · live music Friday nights

A local hotspot with an excellent wine bar — strong local and international list — and live music on Friday nights. Great lower-key spot to gather as a group with a glass in hand. (Your Fri Oct 2 arrival could line up nicely.)

Glasses ~$10–16.

LOUNGEDriftwood Restaurant & Lounge

179 N Hemlock St · later hours · local crowd

Beyond its dinner side (see Food), the Driftwood lounge is a friendly, local late-ish hang with cocktails, whiskey and scotch — a reliable downtown nightcap that isn't a brewery.

BREWERYPelican Brewing — Cannon Beach · kept for GF

1371 S Hemlock St · oceanfront

Only here because it's the safest celiac spot in town — dedicated fryer and rice-flour fried items (GF onion rings, dirty fries, wings) plus some GF drinks, big indoor space, oceanfront. Handy for a group meal where the bachelor needs reliable GF, even if beer isn't the draw.

Food $15–25.

Off-the-Wall & Local Connection

The stuff that makes the trip yours, not a top-10 list.

⭐ UNIQUEGuided wild mushroom foraging hike

Fort Stevens / North Coast · Sept–Nov (your dates land in season!)

October is prime fungi season. Ranger-led mushroom ID hikes (~1 mi) run Sept–Nov — learn foraging rules and identification in the coastal forest. The North Coast Food Trail also connects foraging experiences across Astoria–Seaside–Cannon Beach. A memorable, very-Oregon group morning.

Check the Fort Stevens / Travel Astoria calendar for Oct dates and reserve.

⭐ UNIQUERazor clamming near Astoria/Warrenton

Fort Clatsop to Seaside flats · ~30–45 min

The 18 miles of flat beaches up here are some of the best clamming in the state — and the closed season (Jul 15–Sep 30) reopens Oct 1, so your dates are perfect. Grab cheap licenses + clam guns, dig at low tide, and you've got a story (and dinner).

Shellfish license ~$10–12; clam gun rental cheap.

⭐ DAY TRIPAstoria afternoon

~35 min north · river town with character

Historic riverfront town (yes, The Goonies): Vineside wine bar, brewpubs, the Astoria Column, riverwalk, and great old architecture. A fun half-day with built-in food/wine stops.

Events Around Oct 2–5, 2026

Savor Cannon Beach — Wine & Culinary Festival

The town's signature food-and-wine festival happens across October — multiple days of NW wine tastings, culinary events, and a wine walk. Sources show it running mid-to-late October in recent years, so it may start just after your dates. Verify the exact 2026 schedule at savorcannonbeach.com — if it overlaps even a day, grab wine-walk tickets; it's tailor-made for your group.

Other timing notes

The Cannon Beach Cottage Tour (homes, design, wine) is Sept 11–13 — just before you. Stormy Weather Arts Festival is Nov 6–8 — just after. Check the town's weekly events calendar closer to the date for live music and pop-ups during your stay.
