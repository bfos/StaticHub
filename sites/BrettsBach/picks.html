Our Cannon Beach / Arch Cape Picks

Bachelor weekend on the Oregon Coast · October 2–5

Staying in Arch Cape, just south of Cannon Beach. Here's the shortlist — pick what sounds good!

🏖 Right Around Arch Cape

Arch Cape BeachRight out the door — miles of quiet, uncrowded sand at low tide. Perfect for a morning or sunset walk.
Hug Point State Recreation SiteAbout a mile north up the beach: a waterfall onto the sand, sea caves, and tide pools. Low tide only — and worth timing for.

🍽 Food & Dining

The Wayfarer Restaurant & LoungeSeasonal Northwest seafood with Haystack Rock views — the standout group dinner. 1190 Pacific Dr, Cannon Beach.

☕ Coffee Shops

Bald Eagle Coffee HouseCozy, independent, 30+ years — books, instruments, and art on the walls. 1064 S Hemlock St, Cannon Beach.
Insomnia Coffee CoA cozy little roastery one block from the beach, with a fireplace to curl up by. Cannon Beach.
Manzanita CoffeeLaid-back independent right on Laneda Ave. ~12 min south of Arch Cape.
Controversial CoffeeQueer-owned local favorite full of personality, in downtown Seaside. ~20 min north.
Seaside Coffee RoastersLiving-room vibe brewing their own in-house roast. 5 N Holladay Dr, Seaside. ~20 min north.
Coffee GirlCafé out on a historic river pier with big Columbia views. Pier 39, Astoria. ~35 min north.

🍷 Wine

Willamette Valley private wine tour ★ recommendedPinot country in the Dundee/Newberg area, ~90 min inland — ideally done on the PDX travel day.
The Wine Shack & Puffin Tasting RoomWalkable in-town tasting room, open daily from noon. 124 N Hemlock St, Cannon Beach.
The Winery at ManzanitaCozy coastal wine bar. 387 Laneda Ave, Manzanita. ~20 min south.
Vineside Wine BarCozy wine bar with Northwest and European pours, in Astoria. ~35 min north.

🥾 Outdoors & Hikes

Ecola State Park — Clatsop Loop / Indian BeachThe iconic Oregon Coast view. ~10 min north of town.
Cape Falcon — Oswald West State ParkBest old-growth-and-ocean combo around. ~15 min south.
Haystack Rock private tide-pool tourNaturalist-guided walk through the marine garden, right on the beach in town. Low tide only, Mar–Oct.
Surf lessons — Indian Beach or Short SandsPrivate group lessons, 1.5–2 hr, via Cannon Beach Surf Lessons & Rentals.

📚 Books, Art & Design

Jupiter's Rare & Used BooksA literary gem across from Downtown City Park. 244 N Spruce St.
Cannon Beach Book CompanyThe classic browser's bookstore. 130 N Hemlock St.
Hemlock Street galleries + live glassblowingA self-guided gallery stroll through downtown.
Flavors of Cannon Beach guided walkA ~3.5-hr guided walk blending galleries, shops, and tastings downtown.

🍸 Nightlife & Drinks

The Wayfarer LoungeOceanfront cocktail lounge with a fireplace, at the Stephanie Inn.
Sweet Basil's Café wine barDowntown wine bar with live music on Friday nights.
Pelican Brewing — Cannon BeachOceanfront spot and the safest gluten-free option in town. 1371 S Hemlock St.
