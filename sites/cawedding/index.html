Getting your bearings

Guerneville sits on the Russian River in western Sonoma County. From your hotel, almost everything fans out along four routes: east up River Road toward Forestville & Sebastopol, northeast along Westside Road to the Healdsburg wineries, west down to the coast, and south over the hills to Occidental. Cazadero (the wedding) is a quick run northwest. Knowing the clusters below keeps you from criss-crossing — group your stops by area and you'll barely drive.

① In-town Guerneville & right next door

0–5 min · walk or short drive

Your zero-effort zone. On Main St: Coffee Bazaar, the Guerneville Taco Truck, and El Barrio. Five minutes out: Armstrong Redwoods (the big easy walk) and Korbel (free bubbly + gardens). Best for arrival evening and the light Saturday.

② East: Forestville → Green Valley → Sebastopol

10–30 min · River Rd / Hwy 116

Heading east you hit Forestville first (Sunshine Coffee, Canneti, Burke's Canoe, ~10 min), then Iron Horse in Green Valley (~20 min), then Sebastopol & The Barlow (~30 min: Khom Loi, Handline, Retrograde). A natural eat + sip + stroll day.

③ Northeast: Westside Road & Healdsburg

30–40 min · Westside Rd

The scenic wine road. Gary Farrell and MacRostie sit along Westside Rd and lead right into Healdsburg Plaza (best shopping/strolling) and Bravas tapas. Pair two wineries + the plaza + a patio lunch into one easy loop.

④ West: the Lower River & Sonoma Coast

15–40 min · Hwy 116 to Hwy 1

Follow the river to the ocean: tiny Duncans Mills (~15 min), then Jenner with Goat Rock / Kortum Trail (~35 min), and Bodega Head (~40 min). The big-views day — save it for Friday.

⑤ South: Occidental (Bohemian Hwy)

~20 min · Bohemian Hwy

A storybook two-block village in the redwoods, on a lovely back road that also points toward Cazadero. Quick, charming stretch-your-legs stop you can tack onto a coast or wine outing.

Quick planning logic

how to batch it

Keep Saturday in zone ①–② (close, short, low-stress before the wedding). Use Friday for one bigger zone — ③ (wine + Healdsburg) or ④ (coast/canoe). Sunday on the way out: ① Korbel/redwoods or a peek at ④ Duncans Mills / ⑤ Occidental.

Suggested rhythm (built around wedding prep time)

Thursday — arrive & settle · zone ①

Get in, take a short stroll through Armstrong Redwoods if there's daylight (5 min away). If you want food, the lunch spots all serve dinner too — Handline, Khom Loi or Bravas — or just grab a margarita and tacos at El Barrio on Main St.

Friday — your big free day · zone ④ or ③ (wedding event that evening in Cazadero)

Do the marquee thing while you have a full window: Burke's Canoe Trips, a Sonoma Coast drive (Bodega Head + Kortum Trail), or two scenic wineries (Iron Horse + Korbel). Coffee at Coffee Bazaar on the way out, quick lunch at the Taco Truck or Khom Loi (lunch from noon). Back by mid-afternoon to get ready — Cazadero is ~20 min.

Saturday — keep it light · zone ①–② (wedding night, more prep time)

Nothing far-flung. Slow coffee at Retrograde or Sunshine, a short Armstrong Redwoods walk or a Healdsburg Plaza stroll, then a relaxed patio lunch (Bravas or Canneti, both do Sat lunch) and back early to get ready in plenty of time.

Sunday — easy out · zone ① / ④ / ⑤

Brunch or coffee (Canneti and El Barrio both do Sunday brunch; Korbel's deli is lovely), the free Korbel garden + bubbles tour, or a quick poke around Duncans Mills / Occidental on your way toward the highway.

Eat Lunches & daytime meals

All serve lunch or daytime hours on your free days — patios, gardens, and an international tilt. Book the marked ones ahead.

Khom Loi — Thai · zone ②

Sebastopol · ~30 min★ ~4.5 · Michelin Bib Gourmand$$ midLunch Fri–Sun 12pm · Reservations rec.

The chefs traveled extensively through Thailand and brought a taste of Chiang Mai to Sonoma. It's a Bib Gourmand pick (great food, good value), and the space itself is a draw — a pretty pond and bamboo lanterns. Lunch service Fri/Sat/Sun from noon makes it perfect for a free afternoon, exactly your international-plus-outdoors sweet spot.

Lunch Fri–Sun 12–9pm (happy hour 3–5); dinner Wed–Sun. Reserve via Resy. Closed Mon & Tue.

Bravas Bar de Tapas — Spanish · zone ③

Healdsburg · ~35 min★ ~4.5 · 1,500+ reviews$$–$$$ shareableOpen 11:30am daily · Reservations rec.

From James Beard finalists Mark & Terri Stark, ranked among the top 20 tapas spots in the U.S. The reason to go is the big back garden: a heated, partly-covered patio strung with lights and an outdoor bar — relaxed and festive for grazing on jamón, gildas and patatas bravas over sherry or vermouth. Opens 11:30am, so it's a natural lazy lunch.

Reserve online (OpenTable); bar seating is first-come. Ideal Saturday-light lunch on the way back from Healdsburg.

Canneti Roadhouse Italiana — Tuscan · zone ②

Forestville · ~10 min★ ~4.4$$$Lunch/brunch Fri–Sun · Reservations rec.

Chef Francesco Torre cooks modern Tuscan with everything made in-house — bread, cured meats, even the mozzarella and fresh pasta. Beyond the plate, a warm, tree-lit patio with a fire pit. Lunch Fri/Sat 11:30–3 and a Sunday brunch make it the closest-to-home daytime option.

Lunch last seating ~2:30pm. Call (707) 887-2232 to book — they turn away walk-ins on busy days.

Handline — Coastal California / seafood · zone ②

Sebastopol · ~30 min★ ~4.4$$ casual order-at-counterOpen 11am daily · No reservations

A modern, airy take on the old roadside drive-in — California-coastal cooking with handmade-tortilla Baja tacos, ceviche, fresh oysters and a fisherman's stew, served on a breezy back patio. Opens 11am every day; quick and low-key when you don't want a sit-down production. A great fast-but-good lunch.

Order at the counter. Easy stop paired with Sebastopol/The Barlow.

Eat Food truck & daytime drinks

The cheap-and-great category — both daytime-friendly and in-town.

Guerneville Taco Truck — Mexican, food truck · zone ①

Guerneville · in town★ ~4.6$ cheap · cash onlyDaytime · walk up

Run by the Vazquez brothers for 30+ years, parked most days in the Safeway lot on Main St. Locals rave about the carne asada and pollo tacos, housemade flour tortillas, and the California burrito (steak + fries). Generous, fast, dirt-cheap, eat-outside good — the kind of food-truck stop that ends up a trip highlight.

Cash only. Perfect quick lunch before an afternoon on the river or the road.

El Barrio — cocktail bar + taco window · zone ①

Guerneville · in town★ ~4.6$$ cocktails / $ tacosTaco window 11:30–2 Wed–Fri · Sun brunch 11–3

A funky-swanky bar specializing in mezcal, tequila and bourbon with inspired craft cocktails and outstanding margaritas. For your daytime schedule, the walk-up Taco Window (Wed–Fri 11:30–2) and the Sunday "Brunchito" (11–3) are the realistic plays — plus an easy Thursday-evening drink since it's right on Main St.

Lounge opens 4pm weekdays; closed Mon & Tue. No driving required from the hotel.

Sip Wineries with great patios & views

Russian River is Pinot & Chardonnay country (plus sparkling). All reservation-friendly, scenic, and outdoors-forward — perfect daytime outings.

Iron Horse Vineyards · zone ②

Green Valley · ~20 min★ ~4.7$35/personReservations required

The most scenic, least pretentious tasting around: the "tasting room" is literally outdoors on a hilltop, estate vineyards spilling away beneath you with sweeping Sonoma views. They make iconic sparkling wines (poured at the White House since 1985) plus estate Chardonnay and Pinot. You can also reserve a picnic table and BYO food.

Hourly slots 10am–4pm Thu–Sun. Book ~2 weeks ahead for weekends; max 6 guests. Standout sunny-morning activity.

Korbel Champagne Cellars · zone ①

Guerneville · ~5 min★ ~4.5 · 600+ reviewsFree tour & tastingNo-fuss / walk-in tasting

Right down River Road and refreshingly unpretentious. The free, hour-long history-and-cellar tour ends with a glass of bubbly, and the flower gardens are genuinely beautiful in late spring/early summer. There's a deli for lunch among the redwoods and blooms — a relaxed, low-commitment outing that's almost in your backyard.

Garden tours Tue–Sun at 1pm & 3pm. Great easy Sunday-on-the-way-out stop.

Gary Farrell Vineyards & Winery · zone ③

Westside Rd · ~35 min★ ~4.6$15 tasting (waived w/ purchase)Reservations rec.

Perched on a hillside off scenic Westside Road, with some of the best valley views you'll get from a tasting patio and serious Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. The drive itself — winding past river and vineyards — is half the pleasure. Pair with MacRostie just down the road for an easy two-stop afternoon.

$15 fee waived with a $100 purchase. Reserve ahead for patio seating.

MacRostie Winery · zone ③

Westside Rd · ~35 min★ ~4.7$20–$55/personReservations required

A sleek glass-and-wood estate with a fountain courtyard and three expansive terraces looking over the vines — built for slowing down. Strong Pinot and Chardonnay program, seated tastings, and it sells out frequently, which tells you how reliably good the experience is.

Reserve in advance; pairs naturally with Gary Farrell on the same Westside Road loop.

Coffee Relaxed coffee shops (with real flavors)

Vibe-first, flavor-forward — exactly your brief. The two roasters below make their own syrups, so flavored drinks are a given.

Retrograde Coffee Roasters · zone ②

Sebastopol · ~30 min★ 4.7$ caféIndoor + patio

A beloved local roaster on Main St. where they hand-make all their syrups and add-ins — seasonal cardamom lattes, rose, even charcoal-and-beet — so you get real, interesting flavors rather than "just the beans." Warm, welcoming, regulars-greeted-like-family energy, with indoor and outdoor seating. Best match for "relaxing vibe + flavors."

Drinks run a bit pricey, but the flavor program is the point. Tasty breakfast sandwiches too.

Sunshine Coffee Roasters · zone ②

Forestville ~10 min · also Sebastopol★ ~4.7$ caféPatio · pet-friendly

Small-batch organic roaster going since 2004, with a friendly, easygoing counter and a signature horchata latte regulars swear by. The Forestville spot is the closest "good flavored latte + relax outside" option to Guerneville, and it's dog-friendly if that matters.

Two locations (Front St., Forestville and Hwy 116, Sebastopol). Grab one en route to the coast or wineries.

Coffee Bazaar · zone ①

Guerneville · in town★ ~4.4 · 900+ reviews$ caféSide patio

The quintessential Guerneville hangout — hometown, unhurried, full of locals — with a relaxing side patio and a deep bench of regulars. It's on Armstrong Woods Rd, literally on the way to the redwoods, so it's the natural morning stop before a walk among the giants. Lattes get steady praise; flavor menu is solid if not as inventive as Retrograde.

Closest of the three to your hotel; easiest grab-and-go before a hike.

Outside Easy, scenic hikes & river time

All easy/relaxed. The redwoods are 5 minutes away; the coast is a beautiful 30–40 min drive. Save the coast/canoe for Friday; keep Saturday short.

Armstrong Redwoods State Natural Reserve · zone ①

Guerneville · ~5 min★ ~4.8 · 1,000+ reviews$10/car · free walk-inEasy · flat

Old-growth coast redwoods right at your doorstep. The Pioneer Nature Trail (~1 mi) and Grove Loop (1.2 mi, ~25 min) are flat, shaded and family-easy, winding past the 1,400-year-old Colonel Armstrong Tree. Cool, quiet, and genuinely awe-inducing — the perfect low-effort, high-payoff walk, and short enough to slot in before Saturday's wedding prep.

Open 8am–sunset. Park free by walking/biking in, or $10/vehicle. Bring a light layer — chilly under the canopy.

Burke's Canoe Trips · zone ②

Forestville · ~12 min★ ~4.6$95/canoeSeasonal (summer)

The classic Russian River experience: a leisurely 10-mile paddle past rock beaches, towering redwoods and clear green water, with otters, herons and turtles along the way. Low-effort flatwater — most people stop to picnic and swim, taking 4–5 hours — and Burke's shuttles you back to your car at the end. No experience needed (just swim ability).

A Friday-sized commitment; not a Saturday-before-wedding activity. Summer season only; reserve ahead on busy weekends.

Bodega Head Loop · zone ④

Bodega Bay · ~40 min★ ~4.8FreeEasy · 1.5 mi loop

A short, easy loop around dramatic headlands with the best whale-watching vantage in Sonoma County and big open-ocean views the whole way. Pairs perfectly with a coast drive and a stop for oysters or chowder in Bodega Bay.

Windy and cooler at the coast — bring a jacket. Best as a Friday outing.

Kortum Trail & Goat Rock (Sonoma Coast) · zone ④

Jenner · ~35 min★ ~4.7FreeEasy · flat, any length

A flat blufftop path above sandy coves and sea stacks — walk as much or as little as you like (the full Shell-to-Blind Beach stretch is ~4 mi round trip). Just south, Goat Rock Beach sits at the mouth of the Russian River and has a resident harbor-seal colony. Spectacular, and easy.

For the most effortless version, the paved Vista Trail loop near Jenner (¾ mi) gives big views with zero strain. No dogs on Goat Rock Beach (seals).

Wander Cute towns to walk & shop

For your "stroll a pretty town" mood. Healdsburg is the most walkable; the little ones are quick, charming pull-overs.

Healdsburg Plaza · zone ③

Healdsburg · ~35 min★ top-rated downtownFree to wanderHalf-day

The region's prettiest walkable downtown — a tree-shaded plaza ringed by independent boutiques, galleries, tasting rooms and some of Wine Country's best cafés and restaurants (Bravas is here). Easy to spend a relaxed half-day: coffee, browse, lunch on a patio, repeat.

Best paired with a Westside Road winery stop or a Bravas lunch.

Occidental · zone ⑤

Occidental · ~20 min★ charmingFree to wanderQuick stop

A storybook two-block village tucked in the redwoods, reached via the lovely Bohemian Highway. A few craft shops, a bakery, and old-Sonoma atmosphere — a short, sweet stroll, and a scenic drive in its own right (it's also roughly on the way toward Cazadero).

Tiny — 30–45 min covers it. Great low-key pairing with a coast or back-roads drive.

Duncans Mills · zone ④

Duncans Mills · ~15 min★ charmingFree to wanderQuick stop

A tiny restored 19th-century railroad town along the lower river — a cluster of weathered wooden buildings now holding antiques, boutiques and a café or two. A quick, photogenic stretch-your-legs stop on the scenic drive between Guerneville and the coast.

Small; combine with Goat Rock/Jenner on a coast day.

Sebastopol & The Barlow · zone ②

Sebastopol · ~25 min★ funky & funFree to wanderHalf-day

Less a classic plaza, more a creative hub: The Barlow is an open-air district of makers, tasting rooms, food and shops (Khom Loi, Handline and Retrograde are all in town). Add the famous quirky junk-art houses on Florence Avenue for a fun, offbeat stroll.

Pairs well with coffee at Retrograde and lunch at Khom Loi or Handline.

FYI Removed / watch-list

For transparency — what I took off and why.

Removed — dinner only (won't fit your evenings): The Lodge at Dawn Ranch (tasting menu, Fri–Mon dinner only — and closed Thursdays), Boon Eat + Drink (opens 4pm) and The Restaurant at Farmhouse Inn (Michelin, dinner only). All terrific if your plans ever open up an evening.

Hazel (Occidental) — beloved, but closed last fall; relocating within Occidental and "reopening this summer" with no firm date. Check when your dates firm up.

Piala (Sebastopol) — lovely Georgian spot, but closing (lease ends end of August). Only consider if confirmed open for your dates.

Big Bottom Market (Guerneville) — the famous "Oprah's biscuits" spot changed hands and is now Piknik Town Market; reviews slipped to ~4.3 and turned mixed under new owners.

Barndiva (Healdsburg) — gorgeous garden, but pivoting away from full dining toward casual suppers/private events; confirm before counting on it.
