Russian River Weekend — Things to Do Around the Wedding

Base: The Surrey Resort, Guerneville · Thursday–Sunday · Wedding events Fri & Sat eve in Cazadero · Rental car

Drive times are from Guerneville. Cazadero (wedding) ≈ 20 min NW. ★ = Google/aggregate rating, verified across multiple review sources.
A note on the 4.8+ bar: coffee shops and casual spots here do hit it, but full-service Wine Country restaurants almost never do — the best, most-loved places in this area sit ~4.4–4.7 with hundreds of reviews. I prioritized the highest-rated local, non-chain spots with strong consistent reviews, real patios/gardens, and an international or food-truck lean. I excluded places with thin or suspicious review histories, and flagged a few recent closures so you don't get caught out. Hours shift seasonally — worth a quick confirm call before you go.
Suggested rhythm (built around wedding prep time)

Thursday — arrive, settle, easy evening

You're in Guerneville, so stay close. A short stroll through Armstrong Redwoods if there's daylight (5 min away), then dinner right in town — The Lodge at Dawn Ranch (in the redwoods) or casual Boon — and a nightcap at El Barrio on Main St.

Friday — your big free day (wedding event that evening in Cazadero)

Do the marquee thing while you have a full window: Burke's Canoe Trips on the river, a Sonoma Coast drive (Bodega Head + Kortum Trail), or two scenic wineries (Iron Horse + Korbel). Coffee at Coffee Bazaar on the way out. Be back by mid-afternoon to get ready; Cazadero is a short 20-min hop.

Saturday — keep it light (wedding night, more prep time needed)

Nothing far-flung. Slow coffee at Retrograde or Sunshine, a short Armstrong Redwoods walk or a Healdsburg Plaza stroll + relaxed lunch (Bravas patio), then back early to get ready in plenty of time.

Sunday — easy out

Brunch or coffee, then the free Korbel garden + bubbles tour, or a quick poke around Duncans Mills / Occidental on your way toward the highway.

Eat Dinners & standout meals

Patios, gardens, and an international tilt. Book the marked ones ahead — small dining rooms fill on wedding-season weekends.

The Lodge at Dawn Ranch

Guerneville · in town★ ~4.7$$$$ tasting menuReservations: strongly rec.

A 7–12 course tasting menu from chefs Ignacio Zuzulich & Juliana Thorpe, drawing on Brazilian, Argentine and Basque influences — playful, hands-on courses in an intimate 40-seat room set among ancient redwoods. Reviewers repeatedly say it "rivals Michelin-starred" places with "absolutely incredible service."

Dinner Fri–Mon, 5:00–8:30pm (closed Tue–Thu). The best "we're already in Guerneville" splurge — ideal for Thursday night.

Khom Loi — Thai

Sebastopol · ~30 min★ ~4.5 · Michelin Bib Gourmand$$ midReservations rec.

The chefs traveled extensively through Thailand and brought a taste of Chiang Mai to Sonoma. It's a Bib Gourmand pick (great food, good value), and the space itself is a draw — a pretty pond and bamboo lanterns make the patio glow at night. Exactly your international-plus-outdoors sweet spot.

Reserve via Resy. Larger parties: email ahead. A genuinely fun, non-stuffy dinner.

Bravas Bar de Tapas — Spanish

Healdsburg · ~35 min★ ~4.5 · 1,500+ reviews$$–$$$ shareableReservations rec. (bar walk-in)

From James Beard finalists Mark & Terri Stark, ranked among the top 20 tapas spots in the U.S. The real reason to go is the big back garden: a heated, partly-covered patio strung with lights and an outdoor bar — relaxed, festive, perfect for grazing on jamón, gildas and patatas bravas over sherry or vermouth.

Reserve online (OpenTable); bar seating is first-come. Great as a Saturday lunch or a Friday-before-Cazadero bite if timing allows.

Canneti Roadhouse Italiana — Tuscan

Forestville · ~10 min★ ~4.4$$$Reservations needed

Chef Francesco Torre cooks modern Tuscan with everything made in-house — bread, cured meats, even the mozzarella and the fresh pasta. The draw beyond the plate is a warm, tree-lit patio with a fire pit; people consistently call it their "special occasion" dinner in the valley. Very close to home base.

They'll turn away walk-ins on busy nights — call (707) 887-2232 to book.

Boon Eat + Drink — California bistro

Guerneville · in town★ ~4.5–4.6 · #1 in town$$$Reservations rec.

The reliable in-town favorite — a small, seasonal California menu (organic produce, sustainably raised meats, sharp small plates) that's "fancy without being stuffy." Walkable from Main St., so no driving on a wedding-prep night. The boon brownie and panna cotta are the signatures.

Closed Mon & Tue; Wed–Sun dinner. Easy walk to El Barrio after.

Handline — Coastal California / seafood

Sebastopol · ~30 min★ ~4.4$$ casual order-at-counterNo reservations

A modern, airy take on the old roadside drive-in — California-coastal cooking with excellent fish tacos, oysters and local produce, served on a breezy back patio. Lower-key and quick, a nice daytime or no-fuss option when you don't want a sit-down production.

Order at the counter; great for a relaxed lunch on a sunny day.

The Restaurant at Farmhouse Inn — Michelin star

Forestville · ~12 min★ ~4.5 · Michelin ★$$$$ (~$200/person)Book weeks ahead

The area's marquee fine-dining room and a longtime Michelin-star holder; courtyard lunch under oak trees and passionfruit vines is lovely. Including it for completeness as the top-end special-occasion choice — reviews are strong but a touch mixed for the price, so go in wanting the full ceremony.

Reservations open weeks out and go fast. If you want one blow-out meal, this or Dawn Ranch.

Eat Food truck & drinks

The cheap-and-great category you asked for.

Guerneville Taco Truck — Mexican, food truck

Guerneville · in town★ ~4.6$ cheap · cash onlyWalk up

Run by the Vazquez brothers for 30+ years, parked most days in the Safeway lot on Main St. Locals rave about the carne asada and pollo tacos, the housemade flour tortillas, and the California burrito (steak + fries). Generous, fast, dirt-cheap, eat-outside good — exactly the kind of food-truck stop that ends up being a trip highlight.

Cash only. Perfect quick lunch before an afternoon on the river.

El Barrio — cocktail bar

Guerneville · in town★ ~4.6$$ cocktailsWalk in

A small, funky-swanky bar specializing in mezcal, tequila and bourbon with inspired craft cocktails and outstanding margaritas (don't skip the guac). Great low-key spot for a pre- or post-dinner drink without driving — it's right on Main St.

Cozy; can get busy on weekend evenings.

Sip Wineries with great patios & views

Russian River is Pinot & Chardonnay country (plus sparkling). All of these are reservation-friendly, scenic, and outdoors-forward.

Iron Horse Vineyards

Sebastopol/Green Valley · ~20 min★ ~4.7$35/personReservations required

The most scenic, least pretentious tasting around: the "tasting room" is literally outdoors on a hilltop, with estate vineyards spilling away beneath you and sweeping Sonoma views. They make iconic sparkling wines (poured at the White House since 1985) plus estate Chardonnay and Pinot. You can also reserve a picnic table and BYO food.

Hourly slots 10am–4pm Thu–Sun. Book ~2 weeks ahead for weekends; max 6 guests. The standout sunny-morning activity.

Korbel Champagne Cellars

Guerneville · ~5 min★ ~4.5 · 600+ reviewsFree tour & tastingNo-fuss / walk-in tasting

Right down River Road and refreshingly unpretentious. The free, hour-long history-and-cellar tour ends with a glass of bubbly, and the flower gardens are genuinely beautiful in late spring/early summer. There's a deli where you can grab lunch among the redwoods and blooms — a relaxed, low-commitment outing that's almost in your backyard.

Garden tours Tue–Sun at 1pm & 3pm. Great easy Sunday-on-the-way-out stop.

Gary Farrell Vineyards & Winery

Healdsburg/Westside Rd · ~35 min★ ~4.6$15 tasting (waived w/ purchase)Reservations rec.

Perched on a hillside off scenic Westside Road, with some of the best valley views you'll get from a tasting patio and serious Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. The drive itself — winding past river and vineyards — is half the pleasure. Pair with MacRostie just down the road for an easy two-stop afternoon.

$15 fee waived with a $100 purchase. Reserve ahead for patio seating.

MacRostie Winery

Healdsburg/Westside Rd · ~35 min★ ~4.7$20–$55/personReservations required

A sleek glass-and-wood estate with a fountain courtyard and three expansive terraces looking over the vines — built for slowing down. Strong Pinot and Chardonnay program, seated tastings, and it sells out frequently, which tells you how reliably good the experience is.

Reserve in advance; pairs naturally with Gary Farrell on the same Westside Road loop.

Coffee Relaxed coffee shops (with real flavors)

Vibe-first, flavor-forward — exactly your brief. The two roasters below make their own syrups, so flavored drinks are a given.

Retrograde Coffee Roasters

Sebastopol · ~30 min★ 4.7$ caféIndoor + patio

A beloved local roaster on Main St. where they hand-make all their syrups and add-ins — seasonal cardamom lattes, rose, even charcoal-and-beet — so you get real, interesting flavors rather than "just the beans." Warm, welcoming, regulars-greeted-like-family energy, with indoor and outdoor seating. Best match for "relaxing vibe + flavors."

Drinks run a bit pricey, but the flavor program is the point. Tasty breakfast sandwiches too.

Sunshine Coffee Roasters

Forestville ~10 min · also Sebastopol★ ~4.7$ caféPatio · pet-friendly

Small-batch organic roaster going since 2004, with a friendly, easygoing counter and a signature horchata latte regulars swear by. The Forestville spot is the closest "good flavored latte + relax outside" option to Guerneville, and it's dog-friendly if that matters.

Two locations (Front St., Forestville and Hwy 116, Sebastopol). Grab one en route to the coast or wineries.

Coffee Bazaar

Guerneville · in town★ ~4.4 · 900+ reviews$ caféSide patio

The quintessential Guerneville hangout — hometown, unhurried, full of locals — with a relaxing side patio and a deep bench of regulars who keep coming back. It's on Armstrong Woods Rd, literally on the way to the redwoods, so it's the natural morning stop before a walk among the giants. Lattes get steady praise; flavor menu is solid if not as inventive as Retrograde.

Closest of the three to your hotel; easiest grab-and-go before a hike.

Outside Easy, scenic hikes & river time

All easy/relaxed. The redwoods are 5 minutes away; the coast is a beautiful 30–40 min drive. Save the coast/canoe for Friday; keep Saturday short.

Armstrong Redwoods State Natural Reserve

Guerneville · ~5 min★ ~4.8 · 1,000+ reviews$10/car · free walk-inEasy · flat

Old-growth coast redwoods right at your doorstep. The Pioneer Nature Trail (~1 mi) and Grove Loop (1.2 mi, ~25 min) are flat, shaded and family-easy, winding past the 1,400-year-old Colonel Armstrong Tree. Cool, quiet, and genuinely awe-inducing — the perfect low-effort, high-payoff walk, and short enough to slot in before Saturday's wedding prep.

Open 8am–sunset. Park free by walking/biking in, or $10/vehicle. Bring a light layer — it's chilly under the canopy.

Burke's Canoe Trips

Forestville · ~12 min★ ~4.6$95/canoeSeasonal (summer)

The classic Russian River experience: a leisurely 10-mile paddle past rock beaches, towering redwoods and clear green water, with otters, herons and turtles along the way. Low-effort flatwater — most people stop to picnic and swim, taking 4–5 hours — and Burke's shuttles you back to your car at the end. No experience needed (just swim ability).

A Friday-sized commitment; not a Saturday-before-wedding activity. Summer season only; reserve ahead on busy weekends.

Bodega Head Loop

Bodega Bay · ~40 min★ ~4.8FreeEasy · 1.5 mi loop

A short, easy loop around dramatic headlands with the best whale-watching vantage in Sonoma County and big open-ocean views the whole way. Pairs perfectly with a coast drive and a stop for oysters or chowder in Bodega Bay.

Windy and cooler at the coast — bring a jacket. Best as a Friday outing.

Kortum Trail & Goat Rock (Sonoma Coast)

Jenner · ~35 min★ ~4.7FreeEasy · flat, do any length

A flat blufftop path above sandy coves and sea stacks — walk as much or as little as you like (the full Shell-to-Blind Beach stretch is ~4 mi round trip). Just south, Goat Rock Beach sits at the mouth of the Russian River and has a resident harbor-seal colony. Spectacular, and easy.

For the most effortless version, the paved Vista Trail loop near Jenner (¾ mi) gives big views with zero strain. No dogs on Goat Rock Beach (seals).

Wander Cute towns to walk & shop

For your "stroll a pretty town" mood. Healdsburg is the most walkable; the little ones are quick, charming pull-overs.

Healdsburg Plaza

Healdsburg · ~35 min★ top-rated downtownFree to wanderHalf-day

The region's prettiest walkable downtown — a tree-shaded plaza ringed by independent boutiques, galleries, tasting rooms and some of Wine Country's best cafés and restaurants (Bravas is here). Easy to spend a relaxed half-day: coffee, browse, lunch on a patio, repeat.

Best paired with a Westside Road winery stop or a Bravas lunch.

Occidental

Occidental · ~20 min★ charmingFree to wanderQuick stop

A storybook two-block village tucked in the redwoods, reached via the lovely Bohemian Highway. A few craft shops, a bakery, and old-Sonoma atmosphere — a short, sweet stroll, and a scenic drive in its own right (it's also roughly on the way toward Cazadero).

Tiny — 30–45 min covers it. Great low-key pairing with a coast or back-roads drive.

Duncans Mills

Duncans Mills · ~15 min★ charmingFree to wanderQuick stop

A tiny restored 19th-century railroad town along the lower river — a cluster of weathered wooden buildings now holding antiques, boutiques and a café or two. A quick, photogenic stretch-your-legs stop on the scenic drive between Guerneville and the coast.

Small; combine with Goat Rock/Jenner on a coast day.

Sebastopol & The Barlow

Sebastopol · ~25 min★ funky & funFree to wanderHalf-day

Less a classic plaza, more a creative hub: The Barlow is an open-air district of makers, tasting rooms, food and shops (Khom Loi, Fern Bar and Retrograde are all in town). Add the famous quirky junk-art houses on Florence Avenue for a fun, offbeat stroll.

Pairs well with coffee at Retrograde and a meal at Khom Loi or Handline.

FYI Skipped / watch-list

So you don't waste a trip — places that came up but I'd hold off on right now.

Hazel (Occidental) — beloved, but closed last fall; relocating within Occidental and "reopening this summer" with no firm date as of now. Worth a quick check when your dates firm up.

Piala (Sebastopol) — the area's only Georgian spot and a real charmer, but it's closing (lease ends end of August). Only consider if it's confirmed open for your dates.

Big Bottom Market (Guerneville) — the famous "Oprah's biscuits" spot changed hands and is now Piknik Town Market; reviews have slipped to ~4.3 and turned mixed under new owners. I left it off the main list.

Barndiva (Healdsburg) — gorgeous garden, but it's pivoting away from full multi-course dining toward casual suppers/private events; confirm what it's actually serving before counting on dinner.
